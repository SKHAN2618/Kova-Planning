01 / PRIVACY STATEMENTKOVA PLANNING CO.

YOUR INFORMATION
MATTERS.

Planning involves people, places and important decisions. We believe the handling of personal information deserves the same clarity and care. This statement explains our website's current operation and how information may be handled when you correspond with KOVA outside the website.

01 / WHO WE ARE

KOVA Planning Co.

KOVA Planning Co. is a founder-led planning consultancy based in Auckland, New Zealand. This statement covers visits to our website and personal information that may be received when people make enquiries or engage with us through separate communication channels.

02 / VISITING THE WEBSITE

What happens when you browse.

Our site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. That hosting activity is separate from KOVA collecting a project enquiry. For details of GitHub's practices, see GitHub's privacy statement.

KOVA has not installed a separate website analytics service, advertising trackers or an online marketing signup system. The site loads the Geist font through Google Fonts and an optional smooth-scrolling library through unpkg; your browser may send those providers technical connection information, including your IP address, when requesting these files. The local enquiry draft is not transmitted to either provider. We will update this statement if our services change.

03 / PROJECT ENQUIRIES

Drafted here. Not automatically sent.

The current project enquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional telephone number, site location, service and project details. It prepares a draft locally in your browser that you can copy or download. Submitting this form does not transmit an enquiry to KOVA, a mailing list or a website database.

Any copy or downloaded file is controlled by you and your device. If you choose to share that draft through a separate email or messaging service, the privacy terms of that service may also apply.

04 / DIRECT CORRESPONDENCE

When you contact KOVA directly.

If you communicate with KOVA outside this website, we may receive contact details and the project information you choose to provide. That information may be used to understand your enquiry, assess the requested services, communicate about a project, coordinate appropriate specialist inputs and maintain necessary business records.

Providing information for an initial enquiry is generally voluntary, but without enough detail we may not be able to assess or respond meaningfully to the request.

05 / SHARING & SECURITY

Only where there is a reason.

Planning work can involve architectural designers, surveyors, engineers, councils and other specialists. Where appropriate for a project, relevant information may need to be shared with those involved, with suitable regard to the purpose of the work, confidentiality and applicable law.

We aim to limit access to information to those who need it, take reasonable steps to protect it, and retain it no longer than needed for legitimate purposes and legal obligations. No online method of communication is entirely risk-free; avoid sending sensitive documents unless an appropriate channel has been agreed.

06 / YOUR RIGHTS

Access, correction and questions.

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, you can ask to access personal information an organisation holds about you and ask for it to be corrected if inaccurate. If you have already communicated with KOVA, please use that existing correspondence channel to make an access, correction or privacy enquiry.

Current website limitation: the online enquiry form only creates a draft; it cannot deliver privacy requests to us. We will publish a direct privacy contact channel when online enquiries are activated. In the meantime, do not assume a message has been received merely because you completed the website form.

You can learn more about your rights through the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

07 / UPDATES

Keeping this current.

We will review this statement when website functionality or personal information practices change. A connected online enquiry service, new analytics or different providers would require this statement to be updated before launch.
