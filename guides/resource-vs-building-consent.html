Resource consent vs building consent.

Two approvals that answer different questions.

Reviewed 16 November 2026

THE QUESTION

These terms are often used together, but they assess different things. Recognising the difference early helps keep planning and design work on the right path.

01 / RESOURCE CONSENT

A resource consent concerns land use, subdivision or an activity that needs approval under applicable resource-management controls. It can involve rules, policies, environmental effects and supporting specialist evidence.

02 / BUILDING CONSENT

A building consent relates to the construction work and whether submitted plans and specifications meet Building Code requirements. Some work may be exempt from a building consent, but Building Code obligations can still apply.

03 / BOTH, ONE OR NEITHER

A proposal may need both approvals, just one, or neither. Permitted development under planning controls is not automatically exempt from building consent, and a resource consent does not replace technical building approval.

04 / THE PRACTICAL QUESTION

Identify the proposed activity, its location, the applicable rules and the building work involved before settling your documentation pathway. Planning and building professionals have distinct roles.
